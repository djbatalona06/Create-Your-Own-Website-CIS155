How to Play

On a phone

  • Left thumb: put it down anywhere on the left side to walk.
  • Right thumb: drag on the right side to turn.
  • Hold FIRE to shoot (you can drag it to aim too). USE opens doors.

On a computer

  • WASD to walk, mouse to turn (click the game first), click to shoot.
  • E or Space to use · 1-3 or Q to swap weapons · Esc to pause.

Dream rules

  • Some doors need a keycard. Some rooms are bigger on the inside.
  • If a hallway feels too familiar, look for something watching you.
  • The Lucid Pill turns everything to cardboard and doubles your damage.
