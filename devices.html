Devices That Run DOOM

A closer look at the unlikely hardware hobbyists have talked into running the 1993 classic.

The "It Runs DOOM" Challenge

Because id Software eventually released the DOOM source code, programmers have spent decades porting it to hardware that was never designed to play games at all. Below are some of the most surprising ports, laid out in a card grid so you can browse them at a glance.

Graphing Calculators

TI-83s and TI-84s used in math classrooms everywhere have been reprogrammed to run DOOM, monster closet and all.

A Digital Pregnancy Test

Its tiny LCD screen and low-power processor turned out to be just enough to render a scaled-down version of the game.

ATMs & Gas Pumps

Both run stripped-down versions of Windows, which is all the excuse a hobbyist needs to get DOOM running on the till.

Smart Appliances

Refrigerators and washing machines with touchscreen controls have enough of a computer inside to boot the game.

Cameras & Printers

Digital cameras and office printers both hide a small embedded OS — and an embedded OS is a DOOM port waiting to happen.

Tractor Dashboards

John Deere's touchscreen displays run Linux under the hood, which is more than enough horsepower for id's 1993 engine.

Handheld Cyberdecks

Tiny hobbyist-built handhelds, cobbled together from Raspberry Pis and spare parts, exist mostly to prove DOOM can run on them.
